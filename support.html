01 / PLAYING

Find a puzzle to enjoy

Choose a mode from the lobby, or tap CONTINUE to return to your current journey. The first level of each mode is available from the start; later levels unlock in order within that mode.

How do the four modes work?

  • Card Grid: choose a reserve card, then an outlined space. Meet every row and column goal.
  • Card Trails: tap five neighboring cards connected up, down, left, or right, then collect them for a matching goal. Collected cards leave the board.
  • Hand Orders: place reserve cards into the outlined order slots. Each card can be used only once. Tap a placed card to return it.
  • Repair Challenge: select two outlined cards and confirm a swap. Meet all goals within the swap limit.

What if I get stuck?

Open LEARN HANDS in the lobby or RULES during a puzzle. You can use a hint, undo a move, or restart the current level. If a trail has no route left or a repair runs out of swaps, the game offers a way to undo or try again.

02 / YOUR COLLECTION

Earn a little more with each puzzle

What do the three badges mean?

Each level has badges for clearing it, finishing without hints, and staying within its action target. Undone actions still count toward that target. Replay to earn missing badges; badges already earned remain yours.

How do I unlock and use card backs?

Open COLLECTION to see eight designs, including your starter. Select a design to see its unlock condition. Clear three different puzzles to earn Rose Window, then select it and tap DISPLAY THIS DESIGN to show it in the lobby. Other designs reward progress in particular modes or badge totals.

03 / SAVED PROGRESS

Come back to your own table

Progress, unfinished boards, badges, card-back choices, and sound settings are saved on your device. The game does not provide account-based cloud synchronization, so progress is not automatically shared between devices.

Restarting a level clears its current arrangement while keeping completed levels and earned collection progress. Deleting the app or clearing its data can remove local saves. Avoid reinstalling as a first troubleshooting step if you want to keep your progress.

If you see a message that progress cannot be saved, contact us and mention that message along with your device model.

04 / TROUBLESHOOTING

Connection and controls

The loading screen asks me to retry.

Check your internet connection, then tap RETRY. The app may need to retrieve startup configuration. If the problem continues, tell us the exact message and approximately when it happened. An internet connection may be needed even when your puzzle progress is stored locally.

Where are sound and support?

Use Settings from the lobby, or Pause during a puzzle. You can change sound effects there. CONTACT SUPPORT shows our email address and an OPEN MAIL APP button. If no email application opens, use the address below in your preferred email service.

Cards or buttons do not fit my screen.

The app is designed for landscape play on iPhone and iPad. Tell us your device model, orientation, puzzle mode, and level. You may attach a screenshot if you choose.
