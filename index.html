01 / ABOUT

A clear view of your information

This policy explains how the individual developer of Cozy Gleam Card (the “Game”) handles information in the iOS and iPadOS app, its online services, and communications with players. “We,” “our,” and “us” refer to that developer, identified in the contact section.

This notice describes our practices. It does not replace any permission or consent required for a particular use of your information.

♣

On your device

Boards, progress, badges, card backs, and sound settings.

♠

For our services

Installation identifiers, session activity, and technical records.

♡

When you contact us

Your email and the details you choose to share.

02 / INFORMATION

What we process

Puzzle progress and preferences

The built-in card puzzles save completed levels, unfinished boards, best results, mastery badges, unlocked and equipped card backs, tutorial progress, and sound preferences on your device. The local save feature does not provide an account or cloud synchronization.

The built-in puzzle modes do not require registration, login, purchases, deposits, or chat. They do not access your contacts, photos, microphone, or precise device location.

Configuration and service information

The app creates an installation-specific identifier and sends it with the game identifier to our configuration service. It can also report the outcome of startup access checks, including a detected VPN or network-tunnel condition. We use these records to provide configuration, determine service availability, and investigate connection or access problems.

When your device connects to our services or measurement providers, they receive network and technical information such as your IP address, request time, device and operating-system information, and app or SDK version. An IP address can indicate an approximate location even though the built-in puzzles do not request precise device location.

Installation, session, and attribution information

When Adjust is active, it processes installation and session activity, permitted device identifiers, and technical metadata. Identifiers can include Apple's Identifier for Vendors (IDFV) and an Adjust identifier. Attribution records describe the source or link associated with an installation or session.

Measurement and diagnostic records may be associated with an installation or device identifier even though the built-in puzzles have no player accounts. More about our use of Adjust appears below.

Messages you send

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments or diagnostic details you choose to include. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, tax identifiers, or other information unrelated to your request.

03 / PURPOSES

Why we use information

  • Provide the Game, continue your locally saved puzzles, and remember your preferences and collection.
  • Deliver configuration and online services, evaluate service access conditions, and troubleshoot failures.
  • Understand installations, sessions, and activity from our own app and links, and improve reliability and the playing experience.
  • Respond to support or privacy requests, protect our services, and meet applicable legal obligations.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we rely on the basis appropriate to the activity: providing a service you request, legitimate interests in operating and improving the Game, legal obligations, or consent where required. You may contact us to ask about the basis relevant to your information.

04 / ANALYTICS

Adjust and device identifiers

We integrate Adjust, provided by Adjust GmbH, to understand installations, sessions, and activity from our own app and links. Adjust processes the measurement information described above and supplies reports on our behalf.

Our use is limited to first-party analytics. We do not use this information to link a player's activity with data from other companies' apps or websites for advertising or advertising attribution, or send device data to advertising platforms.

About IDFA and ATT

The iOS integration disables reading of Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not request App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission. Installation identifiers, session activity, and technical records can still be processed as described here. No ATT prompt does not mean no data collection.

For information about the provider, read the Adjust Privacy Policy. Contact us about processing associated with this Game, including access or deletion requests.

05 / SHARING

Who receives information

Service providers
Providers supporting hosting, configuration, email support, and analytics receive the information needed to perform their services. Adjust is described above. Providers processing data on our behalf are required to protect it and limit its use to authorized purposes, consistently with this policy and applicable requirements.
Legal and safety needs
We may disclose information where required by law, to investigate abuse or protect users and services, or to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.
Apple's services
Apple separately processes information for App Store or TestFlight distribution and related platform services under Apple's Privacy Policy.

06 / STORAGE

Local saves and online content

The Game uses local storage for puzzle saves, preferences, cached configuration, and attribution state. Saving your puzzles locally does not mean that configuration or analytics records stay only on your device.

The app can display online content in an embedded browser. Those services may use cookies or similar storage for service state and preferences. Information processed through services we operate in the Game remains covered by this policy. If an additional feature requires a different use of personal information, the relevant notice and any required consent must be provided before that use.

Independent websites reached through external links explain their own processing in their privacy notices. This does not remove our responsibility for information processed through the Game.

This policy page contains no analytics scripts, advertising tags, forms, or cookies of its own. When it is hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub receives technical request information, including visitors' IP addresses, under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Email links open your email application; any message you send is also handled by your email provider and ours.

07 / RETENTION

Keeping and deleting information

Data on your device

Local progress and settings remain until the associated app data is removed. Deleting the app normally removes that local data. Offloading the app may preserve it, and device backups may retain copies according to your backup settings.

Records held by services

Configuration, access, measurement, and support records are retained for as long as needed for their stated purposes. The period depends on the record type, the time needed to diagnose a problem or analyze service use, an open support request, and applicable legal or security requirements. Records are deleted or anonymized when identifiable information is no longer needed for those purposes.

Uninstalling the app does not automatically delete records already held by us or our providers. You can email us to request deletion. We will review the request, locate relevant records, and coordinate with providers as needed. If a legal requirement prevents deletion of some information, we will explain the limitation.

08 / YOUR CHOICES

You can ask about your data

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of personal information; restrict or object to processing; withdraw consent; and complain to a data-protection authority.

  1. Tell us what you need.Request access, correction, deletion, or information about analytics and diagnostic records by email. You do not need a game account.
  2. Help us find the right records.Name Cozy Gleam Card and, if useful, include your device model and approximate dates of use. We may ask for the minimum additional information needed to locate records and verify the request.
  3. Manage local saves separately.Remove the app and its data to remove local progress, taking backups into account. A server-side deletion request does not erase the save on your device.

The Game does not provide an in-game analytics switch, and ATT permission is not a control for these first-party services. Contact us to object to processing or withdraw consent where applicable; we will explain the options relevant to your request. Withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before it.

09 / PROTECTION

Security and international processing

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, and loss. No method of storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

We and our providers may process information in countries other than the one where you live. Where applicable law restricts international transfers, appropriate safeguards are required, such as an adequacy decision or approved contractual protections. Contact us for information about safeguards relevant to your data.

10 / CHILDREN & UPDATES

Children and changes to this policy

The Game is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed to children. An app-store age rating describes content suitability; it does not establish the legal age for consent to personal-data processing.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information without consent required by applicable law, please contact us. We will investigate and take appropriate steps, including deletion where required.

We may update this policy as the Game or our practices change. The date at the top identifies the latest revision. We will provide additional notice or request consent for material changes where required by applicable law.
